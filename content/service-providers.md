<p>Many companies and people offer services for Apache OFBiz. They help with setup, hosting, training, support, custom development and design.</p>
<p>The community keeps the list of service providers on the OFBiz wiki. It has about 35 providers, with their location and website.</p>
<ul class="iconsList">
  <li><i class="icon-pin"></i> See the list: <a href="https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/OFBIZ/Apache+OFBiz+Service+Providers">Apache OFBiz Service Providers</a></li>
</ul>
<p>The Apache OFBiz project does not endorse or recommend any provider on the list.</p>
<h2>Add your company to the list</h2>
<div class="divider"><span></span></div>
<ul class="iconsList">
