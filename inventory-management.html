Apache OFBiz shows what stock you have, where it is, and what it is worth. It reserves stock for orders and tells you when to buy more. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Who it is for

  • Owners and buyers who need to know what to sell and when to reorder.
  • Businesses with stock in one or more warehouses, such as distributors, manufacturers and online sellers.
  • Businesses that track lots, serial numbers or expiry dates, such as food or device sellers.

What OFBiz does for inventory

Stock on hand and stock available

OFBiz keeps two numbers for every stock item: quantity on hand, the units on the shelf, and available to promise, the units no order has reserved yet. So you always see what you can still sell. Guide: Reserve Stock for Orders in Apache OFBiz

Opening stock

Enter the stock you already have when you start using OFBiz, with its location and cost. Guide: Enter Opening Stock in Apache OFBiz

Stock in several warehouses

Keep stock in as many warehouses as you need. Each order line shows the stock in its own warehouse and in all warehouses. Guide: Set Up Warehouses and Locations in Apache OFBiz

Stock reserved for orders

A sales order reserves stock as soon as it is created. If stock runs short, OFBiz can take the order and mark the rest as backordered. Guide: Reserve Stock for Orders in Apache OFBiz

Backorders

Take orders for more than you have in stock. When new stock arrives, OFBiz gives it to the waiting orders. Guide: Backorders in Apache OFBiz: Selling When Stock Runs Out

Minimum stock and reorder

Set a minimum stock and a reorder quantity for each product in each warehouse. When stock drops below the minimum, OFBiz creates a request to buy more, which you approve and turn into a purchase order. Guides: Set Minimum Stock and Reorder Quantities in Apache OFBiz, Creating Purchase Orders from MRP Recommendations in Apache OFBiz, Purchase Requisitions in Apache OFBiz: From Request to Purchase Order

Lots, serial numbers and expiry dates

Give each batch a lot number and an expiry date, or track single units by serial number. OFBiz can reserve the stock that expires first, and show where each lot went. Guide: Track Lots, Serial Numbers and Expiry Dates in Apache OFBiz

Stock counts and adjustments

Record the difference between the system and the shelf, with a reason such as Damaged or Lost. OFBiz updates the quantity and the stock value in the books. Guide: Count Stock and Adjust Quantities in Apache OFBiz

Stock reports and stock value

See each product's stock on hand, stock available and minimum stock in one list. See the average cost and total value of your stock, and the history of every change. Guide: Read Stock Reports: On Hand, Value and History in Apache OFBiz

Move stock between warehouses

Send stock from one warehouse to another when one runs low. Each transfer has a status, such as En-Route or Complete. Guide: Move Stock Between Warehouses in Apache OFBiz

Dropship from suppliers

Sell products you do not keep in stock. When you save the sales order, OFBiz creates a purchase order, and the supplier ships straight to your customer. Guide: Ship Directly from the Supplier to the Customer (Dropship) in Apache OFBiz

Kits

Sell several products as one kit. A kit is picked from its parts, or built ahead into stock. Guide: Kitting in Apache OFBiz: Selling Several Products as One Kit

Inventory control basics

Learn the main parts of inventory control, and where OFBiz handles each one. Guide: What Is Inventory Control? How Apache OFBiz Helps

How it connects with the rest of OFBiz

Sales orders reserve stock, and purchase orders bring new stock in. Stock counts post to accounting, so stock value in the books matches the shelf. For the daily floor work of receiving, picking and packing, see Warehouse Management.

Try it

  • Open the public demo and go to the Facility application.
  • Download Apache OFBiz and run it on your own computer.
  • Read all the inventory guides on the wiki: Apache OFBiz for Inventory and Warehouse Management.

Questions and answers

Is Apache OFBiz free?

Yes. It is open source under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it, change it and run it at no cost.

Can it track stock in several warehouses?

Yes. Each warehouse has its own stock and its own minimum stock for each product.

Does it handle expiry dates?

Yes. Each batch can have an expiry date, and a store can reserve the stock that expires first.

Does it buy stock for me?

Not on its own. OFBiz creates a request to buy when stock is low. A person approves it and turns it into a purchase order.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

Yes. OFBiz runs on Java, and setup needs some technical work. The service providers list names people who can help.
