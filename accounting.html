Apache OFBiz is open source accounting software. It shares one database with orders, purchasing and stock. Sales, receipts and payments post to the general ledger as the work happens. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Who it is for

  • Finance teams in companies that sell, buy and hold stock.
  • Businesses that want invoices and the ledger to follow orders and shipments.
  • Groups that keep the books for more than one company in one system.

What OFBiz does for accounting

Chart of accounts and general ledger

Set up your chart of accounts once. Then use it for each company you keep books for. The general ledger is the record of every amount posted to each account. Staff can also enter a journal entry by hand. User manual: Accounting, Organization GL Settings

Postings that follow the work

OFBiz writes the ledger entries for you when the work is done. Goods received and goods shipped create entries. So do ready invoices, and payments received or sent. A stock count difference and a production run post too. User manual: Accounting, Organization GL Settings

Sales invoices and card payments

OFBiz creates the sales invoice when an order is packed. With a payment provider connected, a card is authorized when the order is placed and charged when the invoice is ready. Trunk ships no provider connections, so a developer must add one. Guide: Invoice and Capture Payment for a Sales Order in Apache OFBiz

Purchase invoices

When a supplier shipment is fully received, OFBiz prepares the purchase invoice. It uses the received quantities and the prices on the purchase order. Staff check it, fix any differences and approve it. Guide: Managing Purchase Invoices in Apache OFBiz

Pay suppliers

Pay one invoice in full or in parts. Or pay several invoices with one payment. OFBiz can print checks for a group of payments. The money itself moves through your bank. Guide: Making Payments against Purchases in Apache OFBiz

See what customers and suppliers owe

The receivables and payables screens list invoices that are past due or due soon. Each list shows a total. Receivables are amounts customers owe you. Payables are amounts you owe suppliers. User manual: Accounting, Invoices

Bank accounts and reconciliation

Keep each bank account as a financial account. Group received payments into a deposit, and match the bank statement to the ledger. This matching is called reconciliation. User manual: Accounting, Financial Accounts

Sales tax

Set up tax rates by country, state, county and postal code. OFBiz adds the tax lines to each order. The tax posts to its own ledger account. Guide: Set Up Sales Tax for Orders in Apache OFBiz

Fixed assets and depreciation

Keep a record of each machine, vehicle or building, with its cost and maintenance. OFBiz works out its depreciation by straight line or double declining balance. Depreciation is the part of an asset's cost used up each year. User manual: Accounting

Budgets

Write a budget for a period, line by line. Send it for review, then approve or reject it. User manual: Accounting

Financial reports and period close

Run the trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement. Compare two periods side by side, and save most reports as PDF or CSV. An inventory valuation report shows what your stock is worth. When a period is done, close it so no more entries go into it. User manual: Accounting

How it connects with the rest of OFBiz

Orders create sales invoices, and purchase orders create purchase invoices. Stock moves post their value to the ledger. See Order Management, Inventory Management and Warehouse Management.

Try it

  • Open the public demo and go to the Accounting application.
  • Download Apache OFBiz and run it on your own computer.
  • Read the Accounting chapter of the OFBiz user manual.

Questions and answers

Is Apache OFBiz free?

Yes. It is open source under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it, change it and run it at no cost.

Can I use the accounting on its own?

Yes, but it is built to work with orders, purchasing and stock. Most of its value comes from postings that follow that work.

Does it compare my budget with actual spending?

Not on a built-in report today. You can record, review and approve budgets.

Does it file tax returns or download tax rates?

No. You set up the tax rates yourself, and file returns outside OFBiz.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

Yes. OFBiz runs on Java, and setup needs some technical work. An accountant should also map your ledger accounts. The service providers list names people who can help.
