Open Source eCommerce

Apache OFBiz includes an online store for your own website. Customers browse your catalog, fill a cart and check out. Each order lands in the same system as your stock and accounting. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Who it is for

  • Businesses that want their own web store, not a store on someone else's platform.
  • Companies that want web orders, stock and invoices in one system.
  • Businesses that sell to other businesses, with quotes and purchase order numbers.

What the OFBiz online store does

A store front for each store

The online store shows the catalog of one product store. Its prices, promotions, tax and shipping all come from the Catalog application. Customers can choose their language and currency. Where: Catalog > Stores

Browse and search products

Customers browse by category and open a page for each product. They choose a variant, such as color and size. They search by keyword and compare products side by side. Where: online store > category and product pages

Cross-sell and recently viewed

Each product page can show related products, such as accessories or a better model. The store also lists the products the customer looked at last. Where: Catalog > Products > Associations

Cart and checkout

Customers add items, change quantities and check out. They pick a ship-to address and a shipping method. One order can ship to several addresses. Guests can check out without an account, on a single checkout page. Where: online store > cart

Customer accounts and order history

Customers register and keep their addresses and saved payment methods. They see their past orders and the status of each one. They can cancel an item that has not shipped. Where: online store > My Account

Promotions and coupon codes

Promotions set up in the Catalog apply in the cart. Customers enter coupon codes at checkout and see the discount at once. Guide: Set Up Promotions and Coupon Codes in Apache OFBiz

Gift cards

Customers buy gift cards and pay with them. They can check a gift card balance online. Where: online store > My Account

Shopping lists and repeat orders

Customers save products in shopping lists for later. A list can be set to order again on a schedule, such as every month. Guide: Set Up Recurring Orders and Subscriptions in Apache OFBiz

Payment options

Each store chooses which payment types it accepts. Options include card, bank account, gift card, cash on delivery and pay later. Card payments need a connection to your payment provider. Where: Catalog > Stores > Payments

Customer returns

Customers ask to return items from a past order. Staff then receive the return in the Order application and refund the customer. Where: online store > Order History

Business customers

Business customers can enter their own purchase order number. They can turn a cart into a request for a quote. Then they order from the accepted quote. Where: online store > Quotes

Reviews, blog and forum

Customers write product reviews. Each store sets whether staff approve them first. The store can also hold a blog and a forum. Where: Catalog > Reviews

How it connects with the rest of OFBiz

Web orders appear in the same Order application as phone orders. They reserve stock, and the warehouse picks and ships them. Products, prices and promotions come from the Catalog. See Order Management, Catalog Management and Warehouse Management.

Try it

  • Open the public demo store. The demo also has the back office.
  • Download Apache OFBiz and add the ecommerce plugin.
  • Read the order guides on the wiki: Apache OFBiz for Order Management.

Questions and answers

Is Apache OFBiz free?

Yes. It is open source under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it, change it and run it at no cost.

Is the online store part of the main download?

No. It is a plugin, an add-on you install separately. Its orders land in the same Order application.

Can I take card payments?

Yes, but you need a developer to connect your payment provider. OFBiz does not ship ready connections to payment providers today.

Can I change how the store looks?

Yes. The store pages are templates that a web developer can change. Expect design work before you go live.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

Yes. OFBiz runs on Java, and setup needs some technical work. The service providers list names people who can help.
