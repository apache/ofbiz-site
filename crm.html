Apache OFBiz helps a sales team track leads, contacts and sales opportunities, from the first call to the order. Its CRM shares one database with orders, so a won deal becomes an order in the same system. It is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Who it is for

  • Sales teams that sell to businesses and follow each deal through stages.
  • Businesses that want customer records, orders and invoices in one system.
  • Marketing teams that send campaigns to lists of contacts.

What OFBiz does for sales and marketing

Leads

Add a lead in a few seconds with the quick add form, or enter the full details with the company, address and email. You can also create a lead from a vCard, and merge two leads that are the same person. User manual: SFA, Leads

Turn a lead into a contact and an account

When a lead becomes real, convert it. OFBiz turns the person into a contact and the company into an account. User manual: SFA, Leads

Contacts and accounts

Keep each company as an account, and each person as a contact with addresses, phone numbers and email addresses. A contact can be exported as a vCard. User manual: SFA, Accounts and Contacts

Sales opportunities

Follow each deal with an estimated amount, a probability, a close date and the next step. Move it through stages such as Prospect, Proposal, Quoted and Closed. User manual: SFA, Sales Opportunities

Sales forecasts

Set a quota and a forecast amount for each sales period. Compare what the team expects with what it must reach. User manual: SFA

Calls, emails and meetings

Log each call, email and meeting with a customer as an event, and assign follow-up tasks. User manual: SFA

Marketing campaigns and contact lists

Plan marketing campaigns, and build contact lists by hand or by importing contacts. Group customers into segments, and use tracking codes to see which campaign brought each visitor. User manual: Marketing, Contact Lists

Customer requests

Record what a customer asks for, such as a quote, information or support, and follow it until it is answered. Where: Order > Requests

How it connects with the rest of OFBiz

Leads, contacts and accounts are the same customer records that orders and invoices use. When a deal is won, staff enter the order for the same customer. See Order Management.

Try it

  • Open the public demo and go to the SFA application. SFA stands for sales force automation.
  • Download Apache OFBiz and run it on your own computer.
  • Read the SFA and Marketing chapters of the OFBiz user manual.

Questions and answers

Is Apache OFBiz free?

Yes. It is open source under the Apache License 2.0. You can use it, change it and run it at no cost.

Can I use the CRM on its own?

Yes, but it is built to work with the rest of OFBiz. Its customer records are the same ones that orders and invoices use.

Is there a mobile app?

No. OFBiz runs in a web browser.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

Yes. OFBiz runs on Java, and setup needs some technical work. The service providers list names people who can help.

Get help
